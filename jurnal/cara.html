Cara Kami Kerja

Satu proses yang jelas, dari mula sampai siap. A clear process from start to finish.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We start by understanding what you need and the scope of your project.

  2. 02

    Site Visit

    Our team visits the site to assess the space and understand the work required.

  3. 03

    Quotations

    You receive a quotation based on the agreed scope of work.

  4. 04

    Construction

    Once everything is agreed, our team begins the work on site.

  5. 05

    Handover

    When the work is completed, we hand the finished project over to you.

Gambar-gambar ini daripada kerja Bina Haus sendiri, tetapi ia bukan satu projek yang sama dan tiada tarikh. Setiap satu dilabel mengikut apa yang ditunjukkannya: Proses (kerja sedang berjalan), Siap (kerja selesai), Render (reka bentuk 3D).
